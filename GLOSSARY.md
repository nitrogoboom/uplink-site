# Uplink site

The words for how the site's Home page is laid out on desktop screens.

## Language

**Fold**:
A Home section laid out as one screen on a desktop browser window.
_Avoid_: one screen, one-screen section, above the fold

**Fit**:
A fold shows from its heading to its last card without scrolling.
_Avoid_: fits the fold, fits one screen

**Fit floor**:
The smallest browser window a fit is promised for: a page area of 1280×720, not counting the browser's tabs and address bar. Below it a fold may scroll, and must still look right.
_Avoid_: minimum screen, smallest laptop

**Fold scale**:
The named steps of vertical space every fold on Home takes its spacing from, all following the window's height together.
_Avoid_: fold curve, fold padding
