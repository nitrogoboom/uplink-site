3 · Where each colour sits on the wheel

The dashed line runs from the logo blue to its exact opposite (hue 59). Amber sits at 68, only 9° away. That is why blue and amber feel like a pair. Amber isn't just an Overwatch leftover.

4 · One colour becomes a family

Each row keeps one hue and steps the lightness from 50 (lightest) to 950 (darkest). The outlined swatch is the original colour, placed exactly. This is where "more than 2 or 3 colours" comes from.
