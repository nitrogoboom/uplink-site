Why A

It does what you asked with what's there. Home's section fits one screen with room to spare, and the Work page shows five projects where it showed one. The words, the link and the reading order stay the same, so there is nothing new to write or re-check.

B is the next step once results are real. The result line is the best reason to tap, but it is sample copy until there is a real client, and it costs about one card per screen.

Same in both: only phones (under 700 px) change. Tablet and desktop keep today's cards. The whole row stays one link, with a 44 px tap target or more.

Looked at and dropped

  • A sideways carousel (swipe through the cards)Shows one card and a bit at a time: fewer per screen, not more. Home's Services already swipes; two swipers in a row is a lot.
  • Two small cards acrossEach title gets about 150 px, so most wrap to three lines, and the drawings get as small as in A anyway.
  • No drawings on phonesThe shortest rows, but the drawings are the site's look.
