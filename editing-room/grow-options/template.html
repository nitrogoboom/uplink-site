Grow section: four ways to build it

Every mockup below uses the real tower drawing from gen_tower.py. Try each one, on your phone too, then pick a letter and a picture mode.

  • It holds the page. On phones and tablets the stage pins for three passes. Someone scrolling to the footer has to push through it.
  • The picture undersells the story. Each level adds a storey plus a few small pieces, and the camera pulls back at the same time, so the growth reads as the building getting smaller.
  • The point is easy to miss. The section's job is "whatever your size, we fit, and we add what you need next". Today you have to scroll the whole thing to get that.

Pick a level (tabs)

Three tabs: one per team size. The picture and text swap on click. It can step through on its own while on screen and stops the moment someone taps. The common pattern for feature sections on Stripe, Linear and Vercel.

B

How big is your team? (slider)

The visitor drags to their own team size. The office grows to match and the list shows what they get, with the new items marked. It answers "is this for a company my size?" in one move. The seat slider on SaaS pricing pages works the same way.

Scroll story, no lock (sticky picture)

Closest to today. The picture sticks while three cards scroll past at normal speed, and it changes as each card reaches the middle. Nothing holds the page: flick past and it is gone. Apple's product pages use this.

    +

    Fixing the picture

    Works with any option. The bar at the top tries the first two ideas on every mockup.

    Mark what's new

    Each level's new pieces glow amber and their tags turn amber. The eye goes straight to the change. "Highlight what's new" above.

    Keep the camera still

    Today the camera pulls back as things are added, so the office shrinks while it grows. Fix the framing and show what's coming as faint outlines. "Show what's ahead" above.

    One big change per level

    Level 1 is the office. Level 2 should be one obvious thing: the security dome over everything, with the homes inside it. Level 3: the second office and the cloud, joined by a line. Fewer, bigger pieces.

    Show the people

    The story is about team size, but no people are drawn. Small figures at the desks and in the homes (or a seat count on the building) would make "11–50 people" something you see.

    My pick

    B, with "Highlight what's new"

    The slider is the only option where the visitor does something that is about them: they set their own team size and see what they'd get. It costs no scroll, works the same on phone and desktop, and the native slider is accessible as is. Tabs (A) are the safe second choice. If you'd rather keep a scroll story, C keeps today's feel without the lock.

    Whichever you pick, the picture work comes next: one big, obvious change per level, and people in the drawing.
