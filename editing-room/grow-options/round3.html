Fix the shape first

Today's tower is wide for levels 1 and 2, then tall at level 3, so no screen box fits every level and the camera has to zoom out. Here are two ways to re-compose the drawing so it fits. These are rough blocks to judge the layout and the fit, not finished art.

Each one plays once when it comes on screen. The phone and desktop frames run together: tap a box in either one. Round 2 · Round 1

Picture
1

Fixed square diorama

One square plot holds all three levels. The camera never moves, so nothing shrinks: the office gains floors, then the homes and the security ring appear on the same tile, then the second office and the cloud. One drawing serves every screen.

  • Phone: the square fills the width, with room for the words below.
  • Desktop: the square sits beside the words, the way the section sits today.
  • Watch for: at level 1 the office is small on a big empty lot. Try "Show what's ahead".
2

Two cuts: tall for phones, wide for desktop

The same story drawn twice. On phones it grows up: floors stack, homes sit either side, the second office in front, the cloud on top. On desktop it grows sideways across three lots: the office in the middle, the homes on the left, the second office on the right. Each cut fills its screen at every level.

  • Phone: a tall picture fills more of a tall screen.
  • Desktop: a full-width band, with the words and boxes under it.
  • Watch for: two drawings to keep in step. The hero already works this way (gen_office.py writes phone and tablet cuts).
