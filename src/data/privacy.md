## What the site collects, and why

### When you open a page

Every visit goes through Cloudflare, the company that hosts our site. To send you the page, Cloudflare receives:

- your IP address
- your browser's name and version, and your device's system
- the address you asked for, and the page that sent you here, if your browser shares it

We use this to deliver the site, keep it running, and protect it from attacks. We don't use it to find out who you are.

### Visits and page speed

Once a page has loaded, it runs Cloudflare Web Analytics. It tells us which pages people read, roughly which country they're in, what browsers and devices they use, which sites sent them, and how quickly the page loaded.

It uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Cloudflare says it doesn't use your IP address or any other detail to follow you over time or across sites. We only ever see totals, never individual visitors.

If an ad blocker or content blocker stops it, that's fine. The site works the same either way.

### Error reports

Sometimes a page hits a bug, or your browser blocks something because of our security rules. When that happens, your browser sends a short report back to our site so we can fix it.

A report includes:

- the page you were on
- your browser's name and version
- either the error message, with the script and line it came from, or the address that was blocked, with the rule that blocked it

Web addresses are saved without the part after a "?", because that part can hold personal details. A report never includes anything you typed. Like any visit, it also reaches Cloudflare with your IP address.

To stop floods, our site counts how many reports come from one IP address in a short time. That count isn't kept. We read reports only to fix problems.

### Settings saved on your device

If you change a setting, the site remembers it in your browser, so it's the same next time you visit. It saves up to three settings:

| Saved as | What it remembers |
| --- | --- |
| uplink.text | the text size you picked in Settings |
| uplink.shortcuts | whether keyboard shortcuts are on or off |
| uplink-held | whether you paused the animations |

These stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser.

## Forms

Our contact form, the "Book a check-up" form, and the newsletter sign-up don't send anything yet. Nothing you type in them leaves your browser. The rest of this section explains how they'll work once they do.

What each form asks for, and why:

- **Contact form:** your name, work email, company, topic, and message, so we can answer you.
- **Book a check-up:** your name, email, company, team size, and the service you're interested in, so we can set up the check-up.
- **Newsletter:** your email address, so we can send you the newsletter (more on that below).

What happens when you send a form:

1. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not a bot, is filling it in. It looks at signals from your device and browser, such as your IP address and browser details. Most of the time you won't notice it.
2. Our site counts how many forms come from one IP address in a short time, to stop floods. That count isn't kept.
3. Your message is emailed to our inbox through Cloudflare's email service. When we reply, it goes to the email address you gave us.

We don't keep form messages in a database. They live in our email, and we delete them once we no longer need them to help you. Cloudflare keeps a copy of each sent email for about seven days, so we can check it arrived.

Please only fill in what the form asks for. Don't send passwords, health details, or payment information through a form.

## Who handles your data, and where

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts this site and runs our analytics. Once the forms work, it will also run the bot check and send our form emails. Cloudflare works for us, under its own privacy and security terms.

Cloudflare is a US company with servers in many countries. That means a visit may be handled outside Canada, and Cloudflare stores data mainly in the United States and the European Union. While your data is in another country, that country's laws apply, and its courts, police, or security agencies may be able to get it. We stay responsible for your personal information while Cloudflare handles it for us.

We don't share personal information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

## How we protect it

The site only works over a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS). The only code it runs from another company is Cloudflare's analytics, plus its bot check once the forms work. Only a few people at Uplink can open the inbox where form messages arrive.
