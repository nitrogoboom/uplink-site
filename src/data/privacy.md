## What the site collects and why

### When you visit a page

Every request goes through Cloudflare, which hosts the site. To send you the page, Cloudflare receives:

- your IP address
- your browser's user agent (the name and version of your browser and system)
- the address you asked for, and the page that linked you here, if your browser shares it

We use this to deliver the site, keep it working and protect it from attacks. We don't use it to work out who you are.

### Page views and load speed

After a page has loaded, it runs Cloudflare Web Analytics. It tells us which pages are read, roughly where visitors are (country), which browsers and devices they use, which sites sent them, and how fast the page loaded and responded. It uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Cloudflare says it doesn't use your IP address, browser or any other detail to follow you over time or across sites. We see totals, not individual visitors. An ad or content blocker may stop it, and the site works the same either way.

### Error reports

If a page hits a script error, or your browser blocks something under our security rules, your browser sends a short report to our own site. It holds the page address, the error or the blocked address, and your browser's user agent. Like any request, it also reaches Cloudflare with your IP address. It holds nothing you typed. We read these reports only to fix bugs.

### Settings saved on your device

The site saves three settings in your browser's local storage so they stay the same on your next visit:

| Name | What it remembers |
| --- | --- |
| `uplink.text` | the text size you picked in Settings |
| `uplink.shortcuts` | whether keyboard shortcuts are on or off |
| `uplink-held` | whether you paused the animations |

These stay on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

## Forms

Our contact form, the "Book a check-up" form and the newsletter sign-up don't send anything yet. Nothing you type in them leaves your browser. The rest of this section describes how they will work once they send.

What each form asks for:

- **Contact form:** your name, work email, company, topic and message. We use it to answer you.
- **Book a check-up:** your name, email, company, team size and the service you want. We use it to set up the check-up.
- **Newsletter:** your email address. We use it to send the newsletter (see below).

How a form is sent:

1. Before it sends, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not a bot, is using the form. It looks at signals from your device and browser, such as your IP address and browser details. Most of the time it runs without you doing anything.
2. Our site counts how many forms come from one IP address in a short time, to stop floods. That count isn't kept.
3. The form is emailed to our inbox through Cloudflare's email service. If you reply to our answer, it goes to the email address you gave.

We don't keep form entries in a database. They live in our email, and we delete them once we no longer need them to answer you or serve you as a client. Cloudflare keeps a copy of each sent message for about seven days, so we can check it was delivered.

Only fill in what the form asks for. Please don't send passwords, health details or payment information through a form.

## Newsletter

Our newsletter is one email a month about IT threats and fixes. We send it only if you ask for it. We keep a record of when you signed up so we can show you agreed.

Every issue says who sent it, gives our contact details and has a working unsubscribe link. We stop sending within 10 business days of your request, and usually much sooner. You can also unsubscribe by writing to [{privacy}](mailto:{privacy}).

## Who handles the data, and where

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts this site, runs the analytics and the bot check, and will send our form emails. It acts for us, under its own privacy and security terms. Cloudflare is a US company. Its network spans many countries, so a request may be handled outside Canada, and Cloudflare stores data mainly in the United States and the European Union.

While your data is outside Canada, the laws of that country apply, and its courts, police or security agencies may be able to get it. We stay responsible for your personal information when Cloudflare handles it for us.

We don't share personal information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

| What | Who holds it | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request details (IP address, browser, page) | Cloudflare | As long as Cloudflare needs them to run and protect its network |
| Page views and load speed | Cloudflare Web Analytics | Full detail for 7 days, then only as totals |

## How we protect it

The site is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). It runs no code from other sites except Cloudflare's analytics and bot check. Few people at Uplink can open the inbox where form messages arrive.
