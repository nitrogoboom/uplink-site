Built to scale

We grow with you

Start with the basics and add more as your team grows. Same people, same help desk, the whole way.

  1. Level 1 · 1 to 10 people

    Get set up right

    A small team needs things to just work. We set up every laptop, phone and account the same way, and you get one number to call.

    • Help desk for every person
    • Microsoft 365 or Google set up and backed up
    • Devices patched and monitored
  2. Level 2 · 11 to 50 people

    Lock it down

    More people means more logins to protect. We add round-the-clock security and train your staff to spot a bad email.

    • 24/7 threat monitoring
    • Email and phishing defence
    • Staff security training
  3. Level 3 · 51 to 150 people

    Plan ahead

    Bigger teams need a plan. We map every device and licence, link your offices, and review the budget with you each quarter.

    • Multi-site networks
    • Quarterly IT reviews
    • Yearly roadmap and budget
