About

We're your IT company

We are the IT department for businesses that do not want to build one. We look at your setup first, tell you straight what is covered and what is not, then run it for you: help desk, security, cloud and the network underneath.

Everyone who answers your call works here, in our office. We do not hand your tickets to an overseas call centre, and the person who fixes your problem today is the same person who will know your setup next year.

Our story

Fifteen years, one help desk

  1. 2011
