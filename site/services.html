Questions

Things people ask first

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Plans run month to month after a short onboarding period. We would rather keep you by doing good work.

Can you work with the IT person we already have?

Yes. Many clients keep an internal IT lead. We take the day-to-day tickets and the after-hours watch so they can focus on projects.

How fast do you answer?

During business hours a technician picks up the phone directly. Our average first response on tickets is 12 minutes.

What happens during the free check-up?

We scan your network, devices and Microsoft 365 or Google accounts, then walk you through what we found in a 30-minute call. You keep the report either way.

Do you work with Google Workspace?

Yes. We run Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace every day, and can move you from one to the other. Not sure which fits? We lay out the trade-offs plainly.

Do you support French-speaking staff?

Yes. Our help desk takes calls and tickets in English and French.

Not sure which plan fits?

Start with the free check-up. We will tell you what you need and what you can skip.
